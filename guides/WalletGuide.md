# User Guide: Tracking Family Expenses

## Home Analytics
- The Home page shows your **Monthly Spending** progress at a glance.
- The bar turns red if you exceed 80% of your target (default target: $2000).

## Wallet Page
1. **Spending Breakdown**: See which categories are taking up most of your budget (Groceries, Utilities, etc.).
2. **Transaction History**: Scroll through all family spending. Items are grouped by date.
3. **Adding Transactions**:
   - Tap the **(+)** button to add a manual transaction.
   - Or use the **AI Receipt Scanner** for automated logging.
4. **Deleting Transactions**: Admins and Members can delete transactions by swiping or long-pressing (depending on view).

## Desktop View
On a desktop or tablet, the transaction list appears as a structured table for easier auditing.
