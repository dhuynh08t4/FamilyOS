# User Guide: Using AI Smart Scanner

## How to Scan a Receipt
1. Click the **Scan AI** button on the Home or Wallet page.
2. Click the **Select File** button (or tap the camera icon on mobile) to upload a photo of your receipt.
3. Wait for the AI to process the image. You'll see a progress bar.
4. Review the detected details:
   - **Total Amount**: Check if the decimal is correct.
   - **Date**: Ensure the date was read accurately.
   - **Category**: The AI will suggest a category; you can change it if needed.
   - **Notes**: A brief description will be generated.
5. Tap **Save to Family** to log the expense.

## Tips for Best Results
- Ensure the receipt is flat and well-lit.
- Keep the camera steady.
- The AI works best with structured store receipts (grocery stores, restaurants, etc.).
