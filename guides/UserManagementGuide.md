# User Guide: Managing Family Members & Settings

## How to Manage Family Roles (Admins Only)
1. Navigate to the **Settings** page via the bottom navigation.
2. Under the **Family Members** section, you will see a list of everyone in your family.
3. Use the dropdown next to a member's name to change their role:
   - **Admin**: Full access to all settings and financial data.
   - **Member**: Standard access, can add/delete transactions and view wallet.
   - **Kid**: Read-only/limited access (no deletion).

## Configuring AI Settings (Gemini API)
FamilyOS uses AI to scan receipts. You can configure this in two ways:
1. **Global Key**: If you are an Admin, you can set a global key for the entire family.
2. **Personal Key**: Any member can provide their own key to override the global one.
3. To save, enter the key and click the **Save (Disk icon)** button.

## Signing Out
Tap the **Sign Out** button at the bottom of the Settings page to safely log out.
