# User Guide: Communicating with Family

## Family Chat
- Access the chat by tapping the **Chat** icon in the navigation bar.
- All messages are shared with the entire family.

## Sending Messages
1. Type your message in the text box at the bottom.
2. Tap the **Send (Paper-plane)** icon to send.
3. To share a photo:
   - Tap the **Plus (+)** icon next to the text box.
   - Choose an image from your device.
   - The AI won't scan these, they are for sharing only!

## Deleting Messages
- You can delete your own messages by long-pressing on the message bubble (Desktop: hover and click three dots).
