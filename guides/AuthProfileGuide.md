# User Guide: Account & Authentication

## Signing In
1. Enter your registered email (e.g., `dhuynh08t4@gmail.com`).
2. Enter your password (default is `1` if set by Admin).
3. Click **Sign In**.

## Updating Your Profile
1. Go to the **Settings** page.
2. Tap on your name at the top.
3. You can edit your **Full Name**, **Nice Name**, and **Username**.
4. Tap **Save Changes** to update your family identify.

## Signing Out
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Sign Out**.
3. You will be redirected back to the login screen.
